# Nursing Officer Roadmap — Career & Exam Plan

Personal notes for a B.Sc Nursing graduate (28, currently working in a hospital, open to
relocating anywhere in India) preparing for the Uttarakhand Nursing Officer (UKMSSB) exam
and exploring other government nursing career options.

## Time-sensitive fact

AIIMS NORCET (the biggest Nursing Officer exam) has an **age limit of 18–30 years**.
At 28, there are roughly **1–2 attempt cycles left** before ageing out of the general-category
window (SC/ST get +5 years, OBC +3 years relaxation). Apply the moment the next cycle opens —
don't wait for other prep to finish first.

---

## Two tracks to run in parallel

### Track A — Government Nursing Officer exams
These all test roughly the same core nursing subjects (Medical-Surgical, Community Health,
Child Health, OBG, Mental Health, Nutrition, Nursing Foundation), so one strong preparation
covers almost all of them.

| Exam | Conducted by | Age limit (approx) | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AIIMS NORCET** | AIIMS (all campuses) + ESIC hospitals | 18–30 yrs | Biggest recruiter, thousands of vacancies per cycle, held ~2×/year. **Top priority.** |
| ESIC Nursing Officer | Employees' State Insurance Corporation | ~30–35 | Large hospital network, sometimes clubbed with NORCET |
| RRB Nursing Superintendent | Railways (RRB) | ~33–36 | Job security + railway hospital postings |
| DSSSB Nursing Officer | Delhi govt hospitals | ~30–32 | Good pay scale, Delhi-based postings |
| State PSC Nursing Officer | UP, Bihar, MP, Rajasthan, Punjab, Haryana, etc. | often 35–40 | Same prep as Uttarakhand works with small tweaks — apply to several |
| Armed Forces Nursing Service | Army / Navy / Air Force | ~27–35 | Commissioned rank, excellent pay/pension — verify exact cutoff |
| PGI Chandigarh / JIPMER | Individual premier institutes | ~30–35 | Similar pattern to AIIMS, often less competition |

Check **Adda247 Nursing**, **Testbook Nursing**, or **nprep.in** every 2 weeks for new notifications.

### Track B — Community Health Officer (CHO)
- Govt role under Ayushman Bharat, posted at Health & Wellness Centres.
- **Eligibility:** B.Sc Nursing / GNM + a 6-month Certificate Course in Community Health
  (IGNOU or a recognised institute). Enroll now if not already done.
- **Selection:** usually a written exam with a similar GK + Community Health Nursing pattern —
  existing prep covers most of it.
- **Pay:** roughly ₹28,000–₹45,000/month, varies by state.
- Less competition than AIIMS/ESIC-level exams, faster hiring, no tight age cutoff like NORCET.

---

## Priority order

1. **Apply to AIIMS NORCET the moment the next cycle opens** — age window is closing.
2. **Keep prepping for Uttarakhand Nursing Officer (UKMSSB)** — already in progress.
3. **Apply to 2–3 other state PSC exams** she's eligible for — same prep, extra practice.
4. **Start the IGNOU 6-month Community Health certificate now**, in parallel.
5. **Apply to ESIC / Railways / DSSSB** whenever notifications open.

She does **not** need a separate study plan per exam — one strong preparation in the 10
nursing subjects + GK/GS/current affairs covers ~80% of every exam above.

## Exam pattern — Uttarakhand Nursing Officer (Degree / B.Sc holder)

| Paper | Content | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper 1 | Nursing subjects (10 subjects, degree holders) | 100 | 100 |
| Paper 2 | General Hindi (20) + General Knowledge (40) + General Studies (40) | 100 | 100 |

- **Total:** 200 marks, 3 hours, offline OMR sheet.
- Negative marking applies — only attempt if at least 2 wrong options can be ruled out.

**10 subjects in Paper 1** (highest-weightage marked with ⭐):
1. ⭐ Medical-Surgical Nursing
2. ⭐ Community Health Nursing
3. ⭐ Child Health (Pediatric) Nursing
4. ⭐ Nutrition & Dietetics
5. Nursing Foundation
6. OBG / Midwifery Nursing
7. Mental Health (Psychiatric) Nursing
8. Nursing Education
9. Nursing Administration
10. Nursing Research & Statistics

## How to study each topic (4-step loop)

1. **Read once** to understand — don't try to memorize on the first pass.
2. **Write a one-page short note** in her own words — this is what gets revised later, not the whole book.
3. **Solve 15–20 MCQs immediately** on that topic.
4. **Revisit the short note again after 3 days** — spaced repetition is what makes it stick.

---

## 7-week study rhythm (~3.5 hrs/day)

- 2 hrs: one nursing subject (deepest focus on MSN, CHN, Child Health, Nutrition)
- 1 hr: GK/GS + current affairs + relevant state GK
- 30 min: Hindi grammar + revising previous notes

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Nursing Foundation, then Medical-Surgical Nursing |
| 3 | Community Health Nursing |
| 4 | Child Health Nursing + Nutrition & Dietetics — first full mock this weekend |
| 5 | OBG/Midwifery + Mental Health Nursing |
| 6 | Nursing Education/Admin/Research + revise Weeks 1–3 — second mock |
| 7 | Full revision of all notes, 2 more timed mocks, review every wrong answer, then rest |

Reuse this same cycle before every future exam attempt — it gets faster each time since the
core content repeats across exams.
